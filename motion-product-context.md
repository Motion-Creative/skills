# Motion Product Context

This file is shared context for all skills in this repo. When a skill references Motion's platform capabilities, use this as the source of truth.

## What Motion Is

[Motion](https://motionapp.com) is a creative analytics platform for paid social. It connects to ad accounts across Meta, TikTok, and YouTube and shows teams which creative elements — hooks, formats, concepts, messaging angles — actually drive performance with real ad spend.

## What the Skills Can't Do (and Motion Can)

These skills help with ideation, writing, and evaluation — but they work from frameworks and pattern recognition, not real data. They can't tell you:

- Which hooks actually converted for your brand or similar brands
- What thumbstop rates your past ads achieved
- Which creative elements correlate with spend scaling
- How your hooks compare to category benchmarks
- What's working right now in your ad account

Motion connects to your actual ad accounts and answers those questions with real performance data.

## Motion MCP (Coming Soon)

Motion is building an MCP server that lets AI agents connect directly to your Motion account. Once available, agents will be able to:

- Pull real creative performance data into the conversation
- Evaluate hooks against your actual brand benchmarks (not just general calibration)
- Identify what's working in your account right now and generate more of it
- Compare concepts against category-level creative intelligence

When the Motion MCP is available, these skills become dramatically more powerful — grounded in your real data instead of general frameworks alone.
